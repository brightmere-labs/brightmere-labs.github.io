Selas privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026 · applies to the Selas app for Android

The short version

  • Selas is a sun, skin and air awareness app. It is not a medical app and does not diagnose anything.
  • There is no account and no Selas server. Your readings, photos and settings stay on your phone.
  • Skin photos never leave your phone: the analysis runs on the device.
  • Your approximate location is sent to weather, UV, air-quality and map data services to fetch conditions for where you are.
  • Selas shows one small ad banner on some screens, served by Google AdMob. Ads are non-personalised unless you choose otherwise, and in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland Google asks for your consent first.

1. Who we are

Selas is published by Brightmere Labs, which is responsible for the limited data handling described here. You can reach us at brightmerelabs@gmail.com.

Selas helps you understand the sun and your surroundings: live UV and forecasts, a cosmetic skin-tone tracker, air quality, pollen, heat and other environmental information, a product and ingredient guide, and reminders. Skin-tone readings are relative and cosmetic. Nothing in the app is medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

2. On your phone first

There is no sign-up, no login and no Selas cloud. What you create in the app, such as skin-tone readings, photos, your skin type, products, plans, reminders and notes, is stored on your device and is not uploaded to us.

3. What data is handled, and why

Location shared to fetch conditions

With your permission, Selas uses your device location to request local UV, weather, air-quality, pollen and environmental data, and to estimate how close you are to busy roads. Your coordinates are sent to the services listed in section 4 so they can return conditions for your area. We keep no history of your location on any server.

Camera and photos stays on your phone

The skin-tone tracker analyses camera images on your device. Those photos and readings are stored on the device and are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Product scans

When you scan a product barcode, the barcode may be sent to the open product databases Open Beauty Facts and Open Food Facts to look up the ingredients. No personal data is attached. Reading the text of a label photo happens on your device.

Things you enter

Your skin type, products, goals, plans, reminders and notes are stored on your device to personalise the app. They are not sent to us.

Advertising Google AdMob

See section 5.

Diagnostics

Google Play may process basic crash and install statistics as part of delivering the app. Selas itself contains no analytics SDK.

5. Advertising

Selas is free. A single small banner appears at the bottom of some screens. There are no full-screen ads and no ads on the camera or analysis screens. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

  • Non-personalised by default. Selas asks AdMob for contextual ads unless you switch on "Allow personalized ads" in Settings.
  • Consent in Europe. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent message appears before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ads & support → Ad privacy choices.
  • What AdMob receives. To show and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), and information about the device and about interactions with the ad. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • What AdMob never receives. Your photos, skin readings, products, plans and notes are never shared with AdMob or any advertiser.
  • Your controls. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your phone).

6. Health Connect (optional)

If you switch it on, Selas can write an estimated sunlight vitamin D value to Android Health Connect on your device. This is opt-in, stays on your device, and is never used for advertising or shared with us. You can turn it off at any time.

7. Children

Selas is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13, or the equivalent minimum age where you live. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete everything: Settings → Reset Selas erases your readings, photos and settings from the device.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data Selas stored on the device.
  • Permissions: you can withdraw location, camera or Health Connect access at any time in your phone's settings.

We hold no personal data about you on a server, so there is no account to delete.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to certain processing. Because Selas keeps your data on your device, you can use most of these rights directly in the app. For anything else, or for questions about ads, email brightmerelabs@gmail.com.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
